# Context: Espoo, its employment services and the TalentMatch events

Facilitator's research note (verified via web sources, Nov 2026). IDs refer to the
[working index](../00-challenge/challenge-brief.md#working-index-added-by-our-team--not-part-of-the-original-brief).

## 1. Espoo in numbers

- Population **325,716** at the end of 2025 (+4,785 / +1.5 % in 2025). [espoo.fi — Population](https://www.espoo.fi/en/city-and-decision-making/research-and-statistics/population)
- **84,225 foreign-language residents = 25.9 %** of the population; foreign-language speakers made up **84.8 % of 2025 population growth**; 16.8 % are foreign citizens. Most common foreign languages: Russian, Arabic, English, Estonian, Chinese, Farsi, Albanian. [same source]
- Vantaa (29.9 %) and Espoo (25.9 %) have the highest shares of foreign-language speakers in Finland. [vantaa.fi — Aluevertailu](https://www.vantaa.fi/fi/kaupunki-ja-paatoksenteko/tietoa-vantaasta/tilastot-ja-tutkimukset/vantaan-vaesto/aluevertailu)

**So what:** the talent pool the City serves is international and multilingual. English is only the 3rd most common foreign language, so "English-only" is not inclusive either. Non-Finnish CV formats, foreign degrees and language requirements are core matching barriers (P9, P10).

## 2. Public employment services moved to the City (TE24 reform)

- On **1 Jan 2025** responsibility for public employment services moved from state TE Offices to municipalities (45 employment areas). Espoo + Kauniainen form one employment area. [espoo.fi news 11/2024](https://www.espoo.fi/en/news/2024/11/responsibility-employment-services-being-transferred-municipalities-200000-customers-finlands), [Job Market Finland](https://tyomarkkinatori.fi/en/news/tyollisyysalueiden-yhteystiedot-1-1-2025-alkaen-1)
- About **30,000 job-seeking clients** moved from the Uusimaa TE Office to Espoo Employment Services; offices in Sello (Leppävaara) and Piispanportti (Matinkylä), advice at Iso Omena. [espoo.fi news 03/2025](https://www.espoo.fi/en/news/2025/03/employment-services-successfully-launched-espoo-and-kauniainen)
- Employer services include publishing jobs, presenting candidates ("Ehdokashaku yrityksille"), organising recruitment events, financial support and a joint Espoo–Helsinki–Vantaa employer advice line. [espoo.fi — Employment services for employers](https://www.espoo.fi/en/services/employment-services-employers)
- Since autumn 2025 the City has been calling employers proactively (first campaign: companies with 2–7 employees). A new team identifies employers' recruitment and competence needs, and the City co-designs training paths with employers and schools. Its pitch: "effective recruitment services free of charge". [espoo.fi news 09/2025](https://www.espoo.fi/en/news/2025/09/espoo-boosts-employer-services-invite-us-visit)

**So what (P1):** the City now owns both the jobseeker relationship and the employer incentive toolbox. A product can show companies this pool, aggregated and anonymised, before they commit. It can also connect early needs to concrete City support.

## 3. Employer incentives the City controls (levers for P1 and P2)

| Instrument | What | Key conditions (summary) |
|---|---|---|
| Pay subsidy (palkkatuki) | Subsidy for the pay costs of an unemployed jobseeker | Granted by the employment authority; fixed-term, permanent or apprenticeship. [Business Espoo](https://www.espoo.fi/en/business-espoo-helping-companies-thrive/financial-support-and-employment) |
| **Espoo supplement for employment** (Espoo-lisä) | Up to **€800 / month for up to 12 months** | Private or third-sector employer hiring an unemployed Espoo resident with pay subsidy (skills improvement / disability / illness) or the 55+ employment subsidy; ≥ 18 h/week; collective-agreement pay. [Business Espoo](https://www.espoo.fi/en/business-espoo-helping-companies-thrive/espoo-supplement-employment) |
| **Espoo recruitment subsidy** (Espoon rekrytointituki) | **€5,000 one-off** per hire | From 3 Feb 2026. Hire an unemployed Espoo jobseeker who is **under 30 or speaks a foreign language**, unemployed ≥ 3 months, with upper-secondary or higher education. Contract ≥ 12 months at ≥ 80 % working time, max 10 per employer per year, granted **before** the contract starts. [espoo.fi](https://www.espoo.fi/en/rekrytointituki) |
| International recruitment assistance | EURES, work-permit services, International House Helsinki, free English job ads on Work in Finland, national Talent Boost network | Free. [Business Espoo](https://www.espoo.fi/en/business-espoo-helping-companies-thrive/international-recruitment-assistance) |

**Design caution:** eligibility depends on personal characteristics (age, mother tongue, length of unemployment). If eligibility is shown per candidate before any human conversation, companies may select by subsidy, and candidates may feel labelled (P10). Show companies the incentives at **aggregate** level ("a large share of this pool may qualify for €5,000"). Let candidates disclose their own eligibility later, after mutual interest (progressive disclosure).

## 4. The event we are redesigning: TalentMatch (Espoo Talent Hub)

**Format today.** TalentMatch is a series of free recruitment events created by the City of Espoo to connect employers with international talent. Events run online or in person and are either industry-specific or open to all sectors. Each brings **200–1,000 jobseekers and 5–50 employers**, with virtual or face-to-face 1:1 meetings, pitching sessions and keynotes. Everything is in English. [espoo.fi — TalentMatch](https://www.espoo.fi/en/working-life/espoo-talent-hub/talentmatch-recruitment-events)

| Edition | Facts |
|---|---|
| Dec 2020 (virtual) | 40+ companies, 500+ experts from 25+ countries. Run on the Deal Room platform: pre-booked 1:1s, virtual booths, lead collection, "TE-Live" broadcasts. [Duunitori](https://duunitori.fi/tyoelama/talentmatch-espoo-recruitment-event) |
| 2024 | **1,300 registered jobseekers (700+ on Tavata) from 31 countries**, 40+ organisations, 20 webinars, live pitching. New: "expertise area matching", a bigger physical fair at Technopolis Innopoli 2, customer care for companies, and **three registration groups with dedicated time slots**, which doubled in-person attendance and kept queues short. Livestream for online participants. [Organiser's LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/katja-savelcheva_espootalentmatch2024-recruitmentevent-activity-7178290374929498112-fvm5) |
| 28 Mar 2025 (in person, Innopoli 2, Otaniemi) | 300+ highly educated international professionals. Programme: live pitching (9 employers + 9 professionals), transferable-skills workshop, **1:1 meetings booked via a "smart matching platform"**, job fair, and an "explore fair" (companies not hiring now, internships, career services). In-person places **fully booked → waiting list**. Employer time commitment 3–8 h. [Info for professionals](https://www.espoo.fi/en/working-life/espoo-talent-hub/talentmatch-recruitment-events/espoo-talentmatch-recruitment-event-2025-information-professionals), [Info for employers](https://www.espoo.fi/en/working-life/espoo-talent-hub/talentmatch-recruitment-events/espoo-talentmatch-recruitment-event-2025-information-employers) |

**Wider Espoo Talent Hub portfolio:** Hello Espoo, a Competence centre for highly educated immigrants, Career Club, Career Bootcamp, community events (Community Breakfast, Industry Afterworks), HeRoHub (a community of HR professionals) and Deep Tech Talent Forum. [espoo.fi — Espoo Talent Hub](https://www.espoo.fi/en/working-life/espoo-talent-hub)

**So what:**
1. The City already has good formats: pitching, a transferable-skills workshop, staggered registration. Our product should **plug into these formats, not replace them**.
2. The market is lopsided. Talent is abundant (1,000+ registrants, waiting lists), while companies are few (5–50) and short of time. **Company attention is the bottleneck**, so every feature must save recruiters time or raise the value of each conversation (P4, P1).
3. Today's platform matching (e.g. Tavata) is based on self-declared "offer/seek" tags. This is the keyword-level matching the brief calls cumbersome (P7) and that AI-polished profiles can easily game (P6).
4. HeRoHub (the HR community) gives us a ready channel for company co-design and early commitment (P1, P2).

## 5. The City's own AI experience (what is likely to be accepted)

- **AI translation experiment on HelloEspoo.fi** (one year, ended Aug 2026, with the Trust M project; AI translations in about 10 languages, e.g. Arabic, Albanian, Chinese, Farsi, Russian, Ukrainian, Estonian). Lessons: AI translation suits clear, easy-to-understand content; users need guidance before they trust it; some situations still need human translation. [espoo.fi news 09/2026](https://www.espoo.fi/en/news/2026/09/espoos-ai-translation-experiment-has-ended)
- Espoo's **Digital Agenda** runs time-boxed experiments (a few months to a year) co-created with companies. This is the natural route from a Junction prototype to a pilot. [espoo.fi — Digital Agenda](https://www.espoo.fi/en/city-and-decision-making/innovative-espoo/ongoing-development/digital-agenda)

**So what:** multilingual features should follow the "AI draft + human review for critical content" pattern and be clearly labelled. Frame the pilot as a Digital Agenda experiment.
