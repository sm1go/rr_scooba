# How City of Espoo recruitment events work today (Nov 2026)

Web research on 14 Nov 2026 for the event-centred redesign (decision D014). Complements section 4 of `01-context-espoo-and-talentmatch.md`.

## Two families of events

| | **TalentMatch** (Espoo Talent Hub) | **Työnantajatreffit** (Espoo Employment Services) |
|---|---|---|
| Audience | International, mostly highly educated professionals | All jobseekers; often people unemployed for a long time |
| Language | English | Finnish |
| Size | 200–1,000 jobseekers, 5–50 employers; 2024: 1,300 registered, 40+ organisations | 1–10 employers, walk-in |
| Format | Live pitching (9 employers + 9 professionals), transferable-skills workshop, **1:1 meetings booked online via a "smart matching platform"**, job fair, explore fair, livestream | Employer presentation at a fixed time, then **an on-the-spot interview for anyone interested**; advice desk (Business Espoo, Omnia, Employment Services) |
| Registration | Online for both sides; in-person places fully booked → waiting list; 2024 used **three registration groups with their own time slots**, which kept queues short | Usually free entry, no registration; some events require checking eligibility (work try-out, 55+ employment subsidy) beforehand |
| Employer info | Employer list published two weeks before the event (14 Mar for 28 Mar 2025) | Company list "updates" until the event; job details given in the presentation |
| Employer effort | 3–8 hours depending on programme | A presentation and interviews in one afternoon |

Recent examples: Työnantajatreffit at Sello library on 5 Nov 2026 (10 companies incl. cleaning, security and staffing firms, plus entrepreneurship info), at Kierrätyskeskus Nihtisilta on 19 Nov 2026 (work try-outs and 55+ subsidy jobs; interview in Finnish, "basic Finnish needed"), and Työnantajatreffit – Kotoperhe on 26 Nov 2026 (presentation at 12:00, then interviews). TalentMatch went national in 2025 (Finland TalentMatch x Tech & IT, run by the six largest cities, Work in Finland and the Helsinki Chamber of Commerce).

## Where signal is lost today (input for the design)

1. **Employer needs arrive late and thin.** A company list is published two weeks ahead or "updates" until the day; the real role is often explained only in the presentation.
2. **Matching on self-declared tags.** The 1:1 platform matches on keywords and profiles that AI can polish; companies are few and short of time, so their attention is the bottleneck.
3. **Queues instead of conversations.** Walk-in interviews mean everyone queues for everyone; TalentMatch solved part of this with time-slot groups.
4. **Language as a gate.** "Good Finnish" or "basic Finnish" is stated without saying what the language is for.
5. **Eligibility checks are left to the jobseeker** (work try-out, subsidies), so people turn up without knowing whether they qualify.
6. **Little visible follow-up.** Nothing public tells the jobseeker or the city what happened after a conversation.
