Research note for the "looks like a normal job portal" problem. Sources checked 14 Nov 2026.

## What this means for the product

1. **The bottleneck is the number of jobs, not finding them.** With 13–30 unemployed per listed vacancy, a nicer search over the same listings changes little. The product has to bring hidden or not-yet-existing work into view.
2. **Small firms have work but no "job".** Many needs are a few hours a week (invoices, cleaning machines, weekend rush). Job carving plus Espoo's support turns that into a role a first-time employer can afford.
3. **The city pays for long-term unemployment.** The incentive model makes every exit from long-term unemployment directly valuable to Espoo; only aggregate figures may be shown, never a per-person "cost" that could be used to prioritise people.
4. **Try-outs are an existing, legal, trusted path that skips CVs and AI-written applications.** The missing piece is a way to find hosts willing to take a try-out and to make the paperwork easy.
