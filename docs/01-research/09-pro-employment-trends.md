# Pro-employment trends in large European and US cities (Nov 2026)

What cities elsewhere do to get more people hired, and what Espoo Work Map takes from it.
Collected by web search on 14 Nov 2026; figures are as reported by the sources.

## Imported into the app as "fair hiring promises"

| Promise in the app | Where it comes from | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **No CV, no interview** (`open_hiring`): the job goes to people in sign-up order | Greyston Bakery, Yonkers NY (since 1982); brought to the Netherlands by Start Foundation in 2019; Rotterdam council motion (Nov 2023) asks the city to fill part of its own vacancies this way | 150+ Dutch employers have used it, incl. UWV, McDonald's and the Municipality of Zoetermeer (Start Foundation) |
| **Conversation guaranteed** (`guaranteed_talk`): everyone who meets the listed must-haves gets a conversation | UK "Disability Confident" offer-an-interview commitment (DWP, Ofcom, councils) | Voluntary scheme; we make it universal so nobody has to disclose health information |
| **Try the work first** (`tryout`) | Germany: *Maßnahme bei einem Arbeitgeber* (MAG, a few days up to 6 weeks, benefits continue); Finland already has *työkokeilu* (up to 6 months) | Bundesagentur für Arbeit; Työmarkkinatori |
| **Experience counts, no diploma needed** (`skills_first`) | "Tear the paper ceiling": New York, Philadelphia, Minneapolis–St Paul, Chicago (2025 ordinance), Springfield MA (2025); 15 US cities and counties per Opportunity@Work | Philadelphia removed degree requirements on day one (2024) and runs the City College for Municipal Employment |

## Already in the app, now with a stronger argument

- **Pay shown on every job.** NYC requires pay ranges in job ads since Nov 2022. In US surveys, 82% of workers are more likely to apply when pay is shown (SHRM 2023). The **EU Pay Transparency Directive (2023/970)** must be applied by member states by 7 June 2026 and gives applicants a right to know the starting pay or range before the interview. All 158 demo jobs show pay; the employer form warns when pay is missing.

## Policy-level ideas for the pitch (not app features)

- **Job guarantee, Gramatneusiedl/Marienthal, Austria (AMS Lower Austria, MAGMA, Oct 2020–Mar 2024).** Long-term unemployment in the municipality fell to zero by April 2021. Over 3.5 years, eligible people spent on average 555 more days in work, unsubsidised employment rose 17%, and costs fell back after 18 months (Oxford/Vienna evaluation, AMS 2026). Espoo's own cost driver is the same group: people unemployed more than 400 days (see `08-espoo-local-situation-2026.md`).
- **Territoires zéro chômeur de longue durée, France (Paris 18e, 19e, 20e and others).** Job-creation companies hire long-term unemployed residents for useful local work. The 2025 national evaluation finds no harm to local firms, but heavy institutional overhead.
- **Fair-chance hiring, US.** 35+ states and 150+ cities delay criminal-record questions; Spokane (2025) also bans questions about the applicant's address until a provisional offer. Less relevant in Finland, where record checks are already restricted by law.
- **Stockholm Jobbtorg.** Municipal job centres report 66% of participants moving into work or study (2020). Swedish 100%-subsidised "extratjänster" helped most for newly arrived women and long-term unemployed people with disabilities (Arbetsförmedlingen 2024).
