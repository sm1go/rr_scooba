Your money as a garden: the three baskets

The fence = bills

Mandatory and regular spending, like rent. Forecast within 6%.

The pond = cushion

Mandatory but irregular spending: the broken washing machine. Your buffer for surprises.

The flowers = fun

Everyday spending that slips through your fingers. Tap one: €319 on entertainment ≈ almost 20 hours of your work, with receipts for every number.

When money gets tight before rent, the weather turns: a calm warning, never a lecture.

Trustworthy AI: computed, never guessed

Straight answers

Ask "What's safe to spend this week?" and get a straight answer, with zero shame.

Deterministic tools

Every figure is computed by deterministic tools on your own data. The language model never guesses a number.

Honest about limits

Ask it to predict next month's fun spending and it tells the truth: nobody can, it's a coin flip.

You decide

Cut dining out by 20% and watch 6 months play out in your own garden. Drag the slider and a wilting flower blooms again. A simulation, never a prediction.
