Ask for anything

Your criteria, answered with quotes — not guesses.

  • "Public sauna", "near the sea" — venues go to Exa Places for every area
  • "Safe to walk home at night" — Exa Search, a judged and quoted answer per area; the ranking re-sorts
  • One flat, deep — Exa Agent researches building, landlord and risks, verifies the citations, writes viewing questions
  • "Unknown" is an allowed answer
