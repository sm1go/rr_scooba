Muutto

Got the job in the Helsinki region. Now, where to live?

An Exa-powered butler, Pukki, does the digging: it ranks areas by your real commute and daily life, finds live listings, researches the flat and the landlord, and writes the applications.

Every fact links to its source. Nothing is sent without your approval.

The challenge

Exa asks for a personal agent that takes a tedious task off people's hands: find what matters across the web, work out what needs to happen, get the task as close to done as possible, leave the final approval to the user, and demo it on a real case.

The problem

You got the job. Now the hard part: where do you actually live?

Scattered

Listings on Oikotie, Vuokraovi and Etuovi; commute, noise, schools and city plans each on a different site.

Unfamiliar

People moving for work don't know the neighbourhoods, the landlords or the local rules, and often not the language.

Untrusted

Dozens of tabs, but few answers you can check. Generic chatbots guess when they don't know.

Who it is for: people relocating to the Helsinki region for work, to rent or to buy.

One sentence in, a ranked move file out

Describe the move→ Areas ranked→ Live listings→ Deep research→ Applications to approve

About 20–27 seconds

The workplace is geocoded and candidate postal areas (Statistics Finland Paavo) are measured in parallel.

Real commute

Transit, car and bike times (OSRM, Digitransit); drive-time zones along real roads (Valhalla), minute by minute.

Daily life

Shops, schools, daycare, parks and stops (OpenStreetMap, city registers), air quality (FMI), traffic noise (City of Helsinki, Väylävirasto).

What's changing

Local street works, city decisions and plans for the coming years, plus dated resident voices, found with Exa.

Live listings, read properly

From the portals, live

Listings with photos from Oikotie, Vuokraovi and Etuovi through Exa Search + Contents, filtered to your budget and rooms.

Facts from the text

Built year, lift, sauna, energy class, pipe renovation: read from the listing itself, each with its source.

The flat's history

Earlier, already delisted pages of the same flat, so you see how long it has been on the market and what changed.

Ask for anything, get a sourced answer

Your own criteria

"Public sauna", "near the sea", "safe at night": venues go to Exa Places, questions to Exa Search, with a judged, quoted answer for every area. The ranking re-sorts.

Deep research on one flat

Exa Agent looks into the building, the landlord, street works and risks, verifies the citations and gives you questions for the viewing.

"Unknown" is an answer

Every number in the UI names its source. When a source is missing, Muutto says unknown instead of guessing.

Pukki applies for you, you approve

  • For your five best flats, Pukki researches each landlord: tenant requirements, how to apply and reviews, all quoted.
  • It writes a tailored application in Finnish and English from your profile and lists what is still missing.
  • Nothing goes out until you press Approve. In the demo, sending is test-only, to your own inbox.
  • Paste the landlord's reply and it becomes a calendar event for the viewing.
  • An Exa Monitor keeps watching your areas and flags new listings.
