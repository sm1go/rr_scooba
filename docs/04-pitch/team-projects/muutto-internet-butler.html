The challenge

Exa asks for a personal agent that takes tedious tasks off people's hands. It should find what matters across the web, work out what needs to happen and get the task as close to done as it can, leaving the final approval to the user. The demo must run on a real case.

One sentence in, a ranked move file out

Tell Muutto where you will work and what matters to you. It researches in layers:

Layer 1 · Areas

8 neighbourhoods measured in parallel, plus 29 live listings

Layer 2 · The map

Drive-time zones that follow the real road network; click anywhere for a route. Noise layer from official decibel maps, street by street

Layer 3 · The city

City plans and what residents actually say

Layer 4 · One flat

The agent digs into the building, street works and risks, then gives you questions for the viewing

Your criteria, sourced answers, real action

Ask anything

A criterion that is only yours, e.g. "safe to walk home at night". Exa checks every area and the ranking is re-sorted.

Every answer has a source

No dummy data: every number names its source. When data is missing, Muutto says "unknown" instead of guessing.

It acts, you approve

It researches each landlord and drafts 5 tailored applications in Finnish and English. Nothing is sent until you approve.

It keeps watching

An Exa Monitor watches your chosen areas for new listings.
