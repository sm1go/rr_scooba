## Top-3 picks and hero concepts (verbatim)

### PM — Head of Product

**Top 3**

1. **PM-04 Three Signals** — the most literal implementation of the brief's north star, "stronger signal and less noise". It attacks P4 for companies and P6 for both sides with a mechanism backed by peer-reviewed evidence from another congested market, needs no AI (strong J3) and explains itself in 20 seconds.
2. **PM-03 Evidence Card** — it resolves the challenge's central paradox: people use AI because articulating value is hard (P5), which creates synthetic signal (P6). An AI that interviews instead of ghostwriting turns the same technology into a source of authenticity, the product's main talent-side value.
3. **PM-01 Need Interview → Challenge Card** — everything downstream depends on a concrete, early need. It fixes P2 for companies and organisers, moves fairness upstream (P10) and, with PM-02, gives companies a reason to commit early (P1).

My fourth, as a P1 anchor: **PM-08**, because closure data is how the City proves it is a serious pipeline.

**Hero concept**

**TalentMatch Signal — fewer, truer conversations.** One artefact, the Challenge Card, flows through the whole journey and keeps both sides honest. Three weeks out, Nordic Grid Oy (a synthetic SME) completes a 12-minute need interview (PM-01): "native Finnish" becomes "Finnish for customer calls from month 6", and the card goes live. Sam, a synthetic telecom engineer moving into cloud operations, builds an Evidence Card with a coach that only asks questions (PM-03): "I was responsible for monitoring" becomes a specific, defensible story with ESCO skills. In signal week Sam spends one of three signals on Nordic Grid's card (PM-04); the recruiter answers 14 signals instead of skimming 140 requests. On event day the 15-minute slot runs on three questions from Nordic Grid's own card, rated with a thumb (PM-06): evidence produced live, by a person, in front of a person. Within 72 hours Sam gets a real answer; Nordic Grid taps "next step: needs permit" and the City desk returns a permit checklist and a specialist callback (PM-08). The demo is five screens (Need → Proof → Signal → Conversation → Closure) on synthetic data. Judges see AI that interviews rather than ghostwrites, scarcity a bot cannot fake, and humans deciding at every step; Espoo gets early needs, outcome data to win companies, and a pilot path inside its existing TalentMatch series.

### RAI — Responsible AI, Privacy & Legal Lead

**Top 3**

1. **RAI-01 Reveal Ladder.** It removes noise (P4) and bias (P10) at the source, and Finnish evidence shows that anonymity closes the interview gap. It is the privacy-by-design backbone for S1 and S2 (J1).
2. **RAI-02 Mutual-Choice Scheduler.** It replaces AI ranking of people with mutual human choice and a transparent rule. The result is less noise and a guaranteed conversation for every talent, with no high-risk classification, and it can be built in an afternoon (J1, J3).
3. **RAI-03 Quote-Backed Cards.** It is the only credible answer to P6 that does not punish non-native writers. It helps people speak for themselves (P5) and makes evidence, not polished prose, the signal (J2).

Honourable mention: RAI-08, our live J3 demo moment.

**Hero concept**

**Working name: "Mutual — signal without scores."** AI translates, people decide, and nobody is ranked.

Demo (synthetic data, C2): a cold-chain logistics SME pastes last year's job ad. Necessity Lint (RAI-04) flags "native-level Finnish"; rewording it as "Finnish B1 for customer calls" grows the event pool from 9 to 31 cards, three weeks before the event (P2). Amina, a logistics coordinator with a three-year caregiving gap, completes a ten-minute interview in English (RAI-03). Every claim on her card is backed by her own words, and the guard visibly refuses an inflated one (P5, P6). Both sides browse anonymous cards (RAI-01), and a mutual star reveals first names. The Mutual-Choice Scheduler (RAI-02) gives Amina two meetings and the SME four relevant conversations instead of a queue of cold approaches (P4). Finally, the organiser runs the Fairness Twin Lab (RAI-08) on 1,000 twins, swapping "Amina Warsame" for "Anna Virtanen", and shows the flip rate live against the release threshold (P10).

Closing line: no CV uploaded, no score shown, no decision left to AI, and the company met someone its old ad would have filtered out. Nothing ranks people, and everything is built to the high-risk standard that applies from 2 December 2027 (J3).

### STR — CEO & Business Strategist

**Top 3**

1. **STR-01 Pool Proof** answers P1 at the moment of decision with evidence instead of persuasion. It doubles as de-biasing (P10) and exposes nobody. It is the most persuasive 30 seconds we can show officials who hear "you have no suitable candidates" every week.
2. **STR-04 Signal Tokens** is the sharpest answer to the challenge's question about AI, because scarcity cannot be generated. It is proven market design, uses no personal data and no AI, can be built in hours and is memorable on stage ("everyone gets three golden tickets").
3. **STR-03 Incentive Stack** is the moat. LinkedIn, Brella and Duunitori can never attach €5,000, a candidate search or co-funded training to a match, and the evidence-based firewall keeps it fair.

STR-02 and STR-05 hold the rest together: the ladder fixes P2, and the ledger proves value and justifies funding the pilot.

**Path to scale:**
1. A 48h demo.
2. One TalentMatch pilot in 2027, bought as a small procurement under the €60k national threshold ([hankinnat.fi](https://www.hankinnat.fi/mika-julkinen-hankinta/kynnysarvot)).
3. Helsinki and Vantaa, which already share one [employer helpline](https://www.espoo.fi/en/business-espoo-helping-companies-thrive/contacts-employers-and-businesses) with Espoo.
4. The [Talent Boost](https://tem.fi/en/talent-boost-en) network, through an open-source (EUPL) City Kit.

Because the 2026 procurement reform restricts in-house routes ([Valtioneuvosto](https://valtioneuvosto.fi/-/1410877/uusi-hankintalaki-voimaan-lisaa-kilpailua-julkisiin-hankintoihin)), open source with competing providers is the realistic multi-city model. No step needs a high-risk AI conformity assessment before December 2027.

**Hero concept**

**TalentMatch Signal** is a trust-and-incentive layer for the City's existing TalentMatch events, running alongside whatever event platform is procured.

Demo story (synthetic data):
1. A Business Espoo account manager sends a link to the founder of a 30-person photonics SME. In **Pool Proof (STR-01)** she dictates "someone to automate our optical test bench" and sees 41 people. Ticking "fluent Finnish" drops the count to 6, so she switches to "English working language".
2. The **Incentive Stack (STR-03)** shows that the role may qualify for the €5,000 Espoo recruitment support, with the warning "apply before the contract starts".
3. Through the **Commitment Ladder (STR-02)** she submits her Need Card at T-21 and reaches Gold.
4. Amira, a test engineer who has been looking for work for eight months, spends one of her three **Signal Tokens (STR-04)** on the role. The company spends one of its five on her anonymised capability card, and the mutual-token meeting books itself.
5. After the event the **Impact Ledger (STR-05)** sends the company a one-page report (11 token requests, 4 meetings, 2 interviews, 1 hire) and shows the City conversion, incentive uptake and estimated benefit-share savings.

That report is what Business Espoo sends to the next hundred companies.

Pitch line: *"Free stops meaning cheap when it comes with proof, priority and money, and when interest has to be spent rather than generated."*

*T-21 days:* the operations manager of Kuura Logistics (a synthetic Espoo SME) builds her Role Reality Card in 12 minutes (TAL-01): three 90-day outcomes, "English to start, Finnish B1 by month 6", and a drafted taster and questions she approves.

*T-14:* Amina, a supply-chain planner who moved to Espoo with her spouse and already has the right to work, spends one of her Three Signals (TAL-05) on Kuura, with no cover letter.

*Event day:* at the Taster Bar she re-plans Kuura's delivery routes in 15 minutes (TAL-06); the reviewer sees only "#A17", rates three anchors and invites her to a seven-minute structured conversation (TAL-07). The recruiter taps Advance, and later redirects Mikko, a chef changing careers, to the food-tech booth (TAL-08).

*+48 hours:* every conversation has an outcome, and the City can show companies a funnel instead of a headcount.

Demo: three phones on stage (hiring manager, talent, recruiter), with TAL-02, TAL-03 and TAL-09 enriching the same card. Pitch line: "Espoo doesn't try to detect AI. It asks for proof."

### TECH — CTO & AI/ML Lead

**Top 3**

1. **TECH-06 Proof Conversations** — the most direct answer to the challenge (J1): structured live interaction is the hardest signal to fake (P6) and the best-evidenced predictor, and it cuts noise (P4). It needs no AI, so Espoo could run it at the next event.
2. **TECH-01 Need Compiler** (with TECH-10 as its test step) — P2 is the upstream root cause; ten minutes of talk beats a form nobody fills in, and the radar rewards doing it early while answering P1.
3. **TECH-04 Rank Needs, Not People** — makes C1, C3 and P8 structurally easier rather than mitigated, and is our most distinctive demo moment (J2, J3): "your data never left your phone".

**Hero concept**

**Näyttö — proof, not prose.** Working name: Finnish for "demonstration", the word Finnish vocational education uses for proving competence in real work.

A hiring manager spends ten minutes with the **Need Compiler (TECH-01)**; the **Supply Radar (TECH-10)** shows the City's pool *does* hold matching talent once "native Finnish" becomes "Finnish B1 for customer calls" — P1 and P2 answered on one screen. Talent build an **Evidence-Linked Skill Passport (TECH-02)** by telling stories in their own language. It never leaves their phone, which runs **Rank Needs, Not People (TECH-04)** against the signed catalog and explains every match on a symmetric **Fit Sheet (TECH-05)**. At the event, every meeting is an 8-minute **Proof Conversation (TECH-06)**, and a skill shown live becomes a **Witnessed-Skill Credential (TECH-07)** the talent keeps.

Demo story (three minutes): two synthetic twins of the same woman — one with a ChatGPT-polished CV, one writing plain, non-native English. Same facts, same Fit Sheet, same outcome: polish buys nothing, evidence buys everything. Then she demonstrates a skill on stage, and a juror scans her fresh credential.

### UX — Head of Design & User Research

**Top 3**

1. **UX-04 Sparks — "Point, don't write".** The sharpest answer to the brief's central tension (P4 + P6): it removes the task AI mass-produces and replaces it with evidence-linking only a human does meaningfully. Fewer, better approaches for recruiters; an honest, refundable, skills-first shot for talents. Cheap to build, memorable to demo.
2. **UX-02 Problem Cards with Pool Preview.** P2 is the brief's most concrete operational pain; the card is the backbone every other feature reuses, and the preview rewards early briefs while answering P1 with real people.
3. **UX-07 Challenge Tables & Table Tents.** Capability witnessed live, the signal no AI can fake, in a format that sets City events apart from any job board.

UX-01 is the indispensable talent-side twin of UX-02; build them together.

**Hero concept**

**Common Table**: *"Companies bring a problem. Talent brings a story. Espoo sets the table."* The job ad and the CV are replaced by two human-authored objects, the Problem Card (UX-02) and Story Cards (UX-01); everything else connects them.

Demo (three minutes, two phones, one printed tent): Sanna records her memo three weeks early ("our CI pipeline takes 40 minutes"), and the Pool Preview instantly shows 17 people with CI/CD experience. Amira approves her card "cut build time from 30 to 6 minutes" and spends one of five Sparks (UX-04) pairing the two, with no essay. Sanna sees the evidence pair, accepts, and only then sees Amira's name. On the day nobody logs in: Amira's offline ticket sends her to Challenge Table 4 (UX-07), where Sanna watches how she thinks and leaves a "witnessed live" note, then to a structured 1:1 at pod 7. Within 72 hours Amira gets an answer and a next step (UX-10), as does everyone who sparked Sanna. The City finally has early needs, real stories, witnessed signal and closure rates that prove its quality to the next company.
